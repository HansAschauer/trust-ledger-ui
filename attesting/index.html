    Attest claims¶

    Prerequisites

    • The tool runs and shows your paper (install).
    • Your key is registered in the ledger and loaded in your ssh-agent (signing).
    • You have read the claims you are about to attest. The tool helps you remember which ones you read; it cannot read for you.

    An attestation says: this person has examined this statement, in this state, at this level of confidence, for this reason. Each one is bound to the exact state of the statement and of the evidence behind it. If either changes, the attestation becomes stale and the claim shows up again under needs attention.

    Levels¶

    The ledger knows three levels, green, yellow and conjecture. What each means for your paper is for your project to agree on (the ledger itself only demands that a yellow attestation has a reason). A common reading:

    Level Typical use Reason
    green you have checked it and vouch for it optional
    yellow you vouch for it with a reservation or after a lighter check required
    conjecture you record it as a conjecture, not as established optional

    Your project may define a checklist (items such as "re-ran the machine check myself"). Ticked items are part of what is signed.

    One claim¶

    1. Click the claim in the list. Read the statement, the back-translation and the Lean statement, if there are any.
    2. Press Mark as seen (v). The claim gets a ✓ in the list.
    3. Press Attest… (a).
    4. Choose level, enter a reason, tick checklist items, check the key.
    5. Review, then Sign.

    Many claims with one reason¶

    1. Narrow the list: the filter (needs attention, no attestation, stale …) and the search box.
    2. Use Select…: all shown, all stale, all without attestation, shown, not yet seen, or clear selection. You can also tick rows, press x, or use the checkbox in the table header for everything shown.
    3. Read them. Next unseen opens the next selected claim you have not marked; press v when you have read it. The bar shows N selected, M seen.
    4. Press Attest N selected….

    The loop that works

    Filter to stale, Select… → all stale, build a review pack for the selection, read it, mark the claims seen, attest.

    The dialog¶

    • Level and Reason. Templates under the reason field fill it in: the ones from your config and your most recent reasons. One reason applies to the whole batch.
    • Checklist. The items that apply to claims.
    • Key. The registered keys, with a note if one is not in your agent (see).

    Review: what will be signed¶

    Pressing Review asks the server to check everything and show a summary. Nothing is signed yet.

    • the person, the key and its fingerprint, and how many signatures (and agent prompts) to expect;
    • the exact reason text that will be signed;
    • a table of the claims: what each has now, the bound hash (the state being attested), and whether you marked it as seen;
    • problems per claim (a wrong checklist item, for example). Claims with a problem are left out.

    Claims you did not mark as seen are listed in an amber box. You can still sign them, but only after ticking I am attesting claims I have not marked as seen. It is a warning, not a block; the decision is yours. If you change anything in the dialog, the review is discarded and you review again.

    Sign¶

    Sign N attestation(s) starts the job. For each claim in turn, the server

    1. checks that the statement and its evidence are still exactly what the review showed, and skips the claim if not;
    2. asks your ssh-agent for a signature (confirm there if your agent asks) and verifies it;
    3. appends the record to ledger/attestations.toml and records a snapshot.

    A failure on one claim does not stop the others; the result table says what happened to each (attested, skipped, failed). Attested claims leave the selection.

    Undo¶

    Right after a batch, Undo this batch removes exactly the records it appended from attestations.toml, provided nothing else has changed that file since. After that, or after you close the dialog and do something else to the file, remove records by hand.

    What stays in your hands¶

    • Seen is your mark: it is kept in this browser, per claim and state, and is not written to the ledger. It does not prove you read anything; it helps you remember.
    • Each signature is made by your key, through your agent, on your say-so.
    • Every action is logged to ledger-ui.log next to the config file.
